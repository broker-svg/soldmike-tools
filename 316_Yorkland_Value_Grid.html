How the numbers were built

  • Subject: 743 sq ft, 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom, 1 parking and 1 locker from MPAC's Assessment Detail and Floor Area reports, plus GeoWarehouse.
  • Comparables: price ÷ the middle of each listing's sq ft range. Only units in 275 Yorkland are used, so the building, amenities and fees are the same for all of them.
  • Size adjustment: small 1-beds sell near $680 per sq ft, but larger suites sell for less per foot. #802 (700–799 sq ft) is asking about $600, which is why Unit 316's range is set near $590–$606, not $680.
  • Excluded: 30 Herons Hill Way #2310 (2+1, 2 bath, $605,000). Different building.
  • Sources: MPAC (Propertyline) and GeoWarehouse reports, and REALM, Nov 2, 2026. 6 sold (Jul 10 – Oct 16, 2026), 3 active.
