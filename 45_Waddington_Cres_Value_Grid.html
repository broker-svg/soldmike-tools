How the numbers were built

  • Subject: MPAC assessment of $916,000 from GeoWarehouse, unchanged for 2023–2026.
  • Comps: assessment = annual taxes ÷ the Toronto residential tax rate for that tax year. The 2025 rate of 0.7540% is calibrated from Waddington's own bill ($6,907 ÷ $916,000).
  • 2026 taxes: converted at an estimated 0.7714% (2025 + 2.3%). Confirm this against the City's final 2026 rate. A 1% difference in the rate moves every comp's multiple by about 1%.
  • Excluded: 22 Rochelle Cres. Its 2026 taxes of $3,415 are about half the street norm, likely a rebate or deferral, so its multiple isn't usable.
  • Pool: MPAC counts Waddington's outdoor pool in the $916K. Many buyers here pay little or nothing for a pool, which is one reason Waddington sits in the middle of its range rather than the top.
  • Sources: GeoWarehouse report and REALM exports, Sep 29, 2026. 11 sold (Jul 6 – Sep 14, 2026), 12 active.
