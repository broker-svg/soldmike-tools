Why This Strategy Wins
The offer date approach gives us flexibility the other strategies don't.

By listing below market value with a set offer presentation date, we generate maximum exposure, drive multiple buyer showings in a compressed window, and create the competitive tension that pushes final sale price upward. Buyers who recognize the property's true value compete against each other to win it.

If the offer night doesn't deliver our number, we have a clean fallback. We re-list at true market value the next day with no loss of momentum and the property continues to attract buyers at a fair, defensible price.

The alternative — overpricing at $2.5M+ — risks the property going stale. In the current market environment, properties listed too aggressively sit on the market for 60+ days, accumulate negative perception, and ultimately sell for less than they would have if priced correctly from day one.
