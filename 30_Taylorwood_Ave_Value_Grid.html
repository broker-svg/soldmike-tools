How the numbers were built

  • Your home: $658,000 assessment, 2,448 sq ft, 4 bedrooms, 3 full and 1 half bath, built 1991, from MPAC's Assessment Detail and Floor Area reports and GeoWarehouse.
  • Tax rates: taken from 30 Taylorwood's own tax bills: 0.892% (2024) and 0.935% (2025). The 2026 rate (0.957%) is an estimate, 2025 plus 2.3%, until Caledon's final rate is confirmed.
  • Each home's assessment = its taxes ÷ that year's rate. Its multiple = sale or asking price ÷ assessment.
  • Outlier: 3 Natureview Crt pays the lowest taxes of the group but asks the highest price ($1.649M), which works out to 2.98×. It's shown on the chart but left out of the averages.
  • Condition of 30 Taylorwood is based on the owners' description of the renovation and will be confirmed at the walkthrough.
  • Sources: MPAC (Propertyline) and GeoWarehouse reports, and REALM, Nov 8, 2026. 6 sold (Aug 25 – Nov 4, 2026), 7 for sale.
