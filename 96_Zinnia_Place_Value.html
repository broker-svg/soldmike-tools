The short version

This house is bigger than almost everything that has sold around it lately. Homes of this size in this area normally start around $1.3 million, even with no upgrades. A house like this one, at about 2,800 sq ft and ready to move in, would usually sell for $1,325,000 to almost $1,400,000.

Two things pull the price down: the location stigma and the renovation condition. The stigma on this pocket keeps some buyers away. Some of the upgrade work also isn't done to a high standard, and parts of it need a closer look. Fewer buyers means less competition, and that's where you get the better deal.

The trade-off

What you accept

  • The location stigma, which can also affect resale
  • Renovation work that isn't well done, and may need fixing later
  • A few red flags that need a proper home inspection

What you get

  • About 2,800 sq ft, bigger than nearly every recent sale
  • A move-in ready house with a basement in good shape
  • A convenient, safe location
  • A price near what smaller renovated homes sold for

If you're comfortable with the location stigma and possible future renovations, the extra space is the most valuable thing about this house.
