How the numbers were built

  • Subject: MPAC assessment of $690,000 from GeoWarehouse and MPAC, unchanged for 2020–2026. MPAC floor area: 1,736 sq ft above grade.
  • Comps: assessment = annual taxes ÷ the Vaughan residential tax rate for that tax year. The 2025 rate of 0.7328% is calibrated from Janus's own bill ($5,056 ÷ $690,000); 2024 is 0.7138%.
  • 2026 taxes: converted at an estimated 0.7496% (2025 + 2.3%). Confirm this against the City's final 2026 rate. A 1% difference in the rate moves every comp's multiple by about 1%.
  • Excluded: 8071 Kipling Ave (small 1,100–1,500 sq ft home, $699K), 15 Fran Dr (priced as a lot to rebuild), 153 Clarence St (1925 1½-storey), and the older 70-day listing of 8555 Martin Grove (it has since relisted, and only the current price is used). 109 Woodcroft's taxes look low for its size, so its 2.06× may be overstated.
  • Size: Janus's assessment is about the same as 2 Janus, 21 Diamond and 130 Jackman, which are all 2,000–2,500 sq ft 4-bedroom homes. MPAC is giving credit for the corner lot and park, which is why Janus sits in the lower half of each tier.
  • Sources: GeoWarehouse report, MPAC assessment and floor-area reports, REALM exports, Oct 9, 2026. 10 sold (Jul 2 – Sep 30, 2026), 20 active or conditional.
